About the Association

Our History & Heritage

Mengo Senior School is the oldest school in Uganda, established in 1895 by the Church Missionary Society. MOSA was created to unite generations of alumni (Bannamengo) who have walked through its prestigious gates on Namirembe Hill.

Our Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and globally connected alumni association in East Africa, ensuring Mengo Senior School remains a premier institution of academic and leadership excellence.

Core Values

Grounded in the school's identity: Fear of God, Integrity, and Respect of Persons & Property. We strive to embody these values in all alumni engagements and projects.

What We Do

School Infrastructure Support

MOSA mobilizes resources to support major school developments, including contributing to modern learning facilities, sports grounds, and school building expansions.

Mentorship & Career Guidance

Alumni regularly visit Namirembe Hill to provide mentorship, career guidance sessions, and professional networking advice to current S.1–S.6 students.

Bannamengo Reunion Events

We organize annual dinners, sports galas, and networking dinners where old boys and girls reconnect, share business leads, and celebrate their shared history.

Bursaries & Student Welfare

Providing hardship bursaries and financial support to bright but disadvantaged students at Mengo Senior School so they can complete their education.
